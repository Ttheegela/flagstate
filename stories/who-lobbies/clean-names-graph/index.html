How to read it

A listed company lobbying through a clean name. A company on a sanctions or export blacklist rarely signs a lobbying form itself. A subsidiary, a trade group, or a law firm with a clean name signs instead, and the listed parent shows up only in the small print: the filing's "foreign entity" or "affiliated organisation" box, or a company registry.

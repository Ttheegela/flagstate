Semiconductors into Russia: what the records can see.

Every chip figure on these pages exists only because a country chose to report it. Fewer countries report a chip row to Russia each year.

For each year, a country is in one of three states in UN Comtrade: it reported a chip export row to Russia, it reported its trade but with no chip row to Russia, or it has not reported that year at all. A missing row is not proof of zero trade, and recent years fill in as countries report late.

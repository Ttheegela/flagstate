# Shadow fleet: what the records show

**Facts from official lists and government releases are stated as facts, with their source.** Where something is our own inference (timing, shared names, one-ship companies), it is marked **Lead**, with a confidence note and, where it matters, an innocent explanation. How the counts were made is in [METHOD.md](METHOD.md). Counts come from public list files unless another source is named.

**Key to abbreviations**
- **IMO number:** the seven-digit number a ship keeps for life, whatever its name.
- **OFAC:** the US Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control, which keeps the main US sanctions list.
- **E.O.:** a US Executive Order, the legal basis for a US listing.
- **EU act:** an EU regulation published on EUR-Lex, the EU's official law site.
- **KSE:** the Kyiv School of Economics Institute, which tracks Russian oil exports monthly.
- **CREA:** the Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air, an advocacy research group.

## 1. The overlap: 42 tankers on both a Russia list and an Iran list

**Fact (official list files).** Of the 625 ships on the UK's Russia sanctions list, **42** are also on a US Iran-programme list when matched by IMO number. The number depends on the filter:

| Filter | Count |
|---|---|
| US entry carries an Iran programme code (strict) | **42** |
| plus Russia actions that also cite the Iran order E.O. 13846 | 45 |
| plus one ship whose Iran code appears only in a second US file | 46 |
| plus ships the US listed only under its terrorism programme | 54 |

No ship that the US listed only under its Russia programmes is inside the strict 42.

## 2. Who listed first: usually the US, not always

**Fact (UK dates from the UK list; US dates from OpenSanctions exports of the US list, checked against two Treasury releases).** Of the strict 42, the US Iran listing came first for **30** (71%) and the UK Russia listing for **12** (29%). The median gap is 209 days. That middle value is shaped by a group of **13** ships the US listed on 30 July 2025 and the UK on 24 February 2026. The spread runs from BODHI (UK first by 477 days) to BLUEBELL (US first by 501 days). Often, but not always, the US Iran listing came first.

## 3. Names change, the hull stays

**Fact (official list files).** **16 of the 42** hulls carry a different name on the UK list than on the US list (13 if US alternative names count).
- The US listed NEW MILOS on 20 March 2025; the UK listed the same IMO number as VOLANS 50 days later.
- JUNIA (UK) is BENEDICT (US); FIORA is GOLDEN EAGLE.
- The UK listed LILY on 21 July 2025; the US listed the same hull as DANYA on 29 July 2026.
- **One hull, three identities (IMO 9282792).** The UK listed CLYDE NOBLE on 9 May 2025 under an unlisted manager. Vicens Marine, a Marshall Islands company, is itself US-listed (6 February 2026, E.O. 13846). OFAC listed the hull as AQUA LIVE on 6 February 2026. The UK entry still shows the old names. (US and UK list files.)

Across all sources, each hull had a median of 4 names (range 2 to 7). Ukraine's war-sanctions ship database is not a sanctions list. It shows different current names for 7 of the 42 but gives no dates, so "renamed after listing" is not shown. FIORA, UK-listed 9 May 2025, appears there under a newer name (Low).

## 4. The managers: young companies, one ship each

**Fact for the counts; Lead for the "shell company" reading, which is not established.**
- No manager of the 42 is UK-listed as a company; the UK lists the ships only. Zulu Ships Management is the only one on the EU list.
- About **28 companies and one individual** are linked to the 42 in the US files (about 37 companies with UK-only managers).
- Of **18** linked companies with a set-up date in the US list file, **10** were set up in 2024 or 2025, 4 to 23 months before their ship's first listing (most 9 to 19 months). Several are named after their ship or one of its former names.
- Zulu Ships Management is linked to **10 of the 42** (8 via the UK manager column, 2 via OFAC links). Fleet Tanqo runs 3. Shanghai Legendary Ship Management (US, 3 December 2024) runs 2 (MIN HANG, VESNA).
- **17 more one-ship managers are US Iran-listed**, usually the same day as their ship, for example Nissho Lines (8 May 2025), Fleet Tanqo (15 April 2026) and Confident Apex (29 July 2026).
- **8 UK-named managers are on no list we hold**; we do not name them. 7 ships have a blank manager field.

Listing-day changes (Medium, one source): Ukraine's database dates owner and manager entries for 10 ships. On 4 IMO-confirmed pages (KAVIZ, HORAE, BRIONT, TAGOR), an entry changed on the US listing day; APATE could not be matched by IMO. For TAGOR the change was to Zulu as owner. Innocent explanation: the database may have updated its record that day.

Treasury's release on Zulu (sb0215, 30 July 2025) says the network's firms "regularly shuffle in and out of management and operator roles."

## 5. Two managers, step by step

### Zulu Ships Management (UAE)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 30 Jul 2025 | OFAC lists Zulu under E.O. 13902 (Iran) and links 11 tankers; Treasury text says it "manages ten vessels" | Treasury sb0215; US list |
| 24 Oct 2025 | EU lists 3 Zulu hulls (TAGOR, BRIONT, MANASLU), Reg. 2025/2033 | EUR-Lex |
| 24 Feb 2026 | UK lists 8 Zulu tankers (Russia regime), still naming Zulu as operator | UK list |
| 16 Jun 2026 | UK lists AETHER and TOA PAYOH, no operator named | UK list |
| 23 Jul 2026 | EU freezes Zulu itself (act 2026/1843 or 2026/1845, unresolved) | EUR-Lex; OpenSanctions EU export |

- The EU listed Zulu's hulls about 9 months before freezing the company.
- The UK has not listed Zulu as a company.
- MISHELL is on the US list only.

### Fleet Tanqo (India)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Registered (year from company number) | US list file |
| 15 Apr 2026 | OFAC lists it (Iran) and names 5 tankers | Treasury sb0443 |
| 16 Jun 2026 | UK lists SILVAR, VERSA, HORAE; only SILVAR's entry names Fleet Tanqo | UK list |
| 24 Jul 2026 | EU lists ANAYA and DAPHNE V, Reg. 2026/1848 | EUR-Lex |

Treasury says these ships carried "more than 20 cargoes of Russian petroleum products in 2025" for an Iran-linked network. The company is not on the UK or EU list by name.

**Across both (High):** 15 of 16 hulls were listed by more countries after the manager's US listing. None of the 16 changed name after listing; renames came earlier (FPMC P DUKE became APATE, FRONT BALDER became TASSOS). The order varied: US, EU, UK for Zulu's 3 EU hulls; US, UK, EU for Fleet Tanqo.

**Russia side (Medium, single investigative source):** the EU lists a manager called Moonlight, alias Avebury Shipmanagement (Reg. 2025/933, 20 May 2025). The UK listed two of its ships, SAGA and SERENADE, on 21 July 2025. The investigative firm Kharon reports that about 14 ships then moved to a new UAE manager, the third company used for the group. That firm is not an entity on any list we hold, so it is not named here.

## 6. The EU's July 2026 act

**Fact (act text on EUR-Lex).**
- **Regulation (EU) 2026/1848** of 23 July 2026 (in force 24 July) adds **41 ships** (entries 652 to 692). By purpose: 27 oil, 6 stolen Ukrainian grain or cultural goods, **5 service ships**, 2 energy and 1 military cargo.
- The service ships fall under a new rule for ships that refuel or tow listed ships: KUMANA, BILAL BEY, BEBEK-E, LADY JASMINE and OCEAN FORTUNE 18. The act gives ship-to-ship transfers as the reason.
- **4** of the 41 were already on the UK Russia list (PORT OLYA-3, LAURYN, SORION, LUCH).
- **5** are on a US Iran list. The US came first for ANAYA, BELLARIS and DAPHNE V by about 100 days, and for VIRENT by 10 days. YEHOPE was listed by the EU first and by the US 5 days later. Gaps are counted from 24 July; from 23 July, add one day.
- A parallel act lists **7 shipping firms**:
  - White Agate Marine (Oman), ship owner
  - Zulu Ships Management (UAE), tanker manager
  - Peninsular Maritime (India), technical manager
  - Astute Maritime (Singapore), technical manager
  - Hua Xia Shipmanagement (China), technical manager
  - Lukoil-Marinbunker (Russia)
  - Aquamarine Ship Management (Russia), crew recruiting
- **Unresolved:** the EUR-Lex page we read is Implementing Regulation **2026/1843**; OpenSanctions cites **2026/1845**.
- Five UAE oil traders went on an EU list in force from 13 August 2026. The UK had listed three of them (Estrella, Arcadia, Linglong) on 24 February 2026, about five months earlier.

## 7. Why this is a climate story

- **Old ships (High).** The 42 were built 1997 to 2009, counting the 37 with a build year (5 blank). KSE figures:
  - July 2026: 150 of 268 tankers carrying Russia's seaborne oil (56%) were shadow fleet, and 93% of those were over 15 years old (via Kyiv Post, 18 September 2026).
  - February 2026: 96% of shadow crude tankers were over 15 years old.
- **Weak insurance (High).**
  - April 2026 (KSE): 30 shadow tankers had Russian insurers: AlfaStrakhovanie 17, Sogaz 10, Balance Insurance 3.
  - All three insurers are listed: AlfaStrakhovanie (UK 25 November 2024, EU 18 December 2023, US), Sogaz (UK 15 March 2022, US) and JSC Balance Insurance (UK 16 June 2026).
  - June 2026 (KSE): 48 shadow tankers had Russian insurance, 42 of them (88%) sanctioned.
- **Spills (Medium to Low).**
  - CREA counted 50 incidents since the full-scale invasion and estimated one spill could cost coastal states $1.6 billion (2024; advocacy group).
  - Bellona reported a Black Sea spill on 3 May 2026 from a 26-year-old tanker with no ice class. Only 28 tonnes were recovered, and an environmental group identified the ship.
  - A newsletter citing Reuters reports that Sweden seized a tanker on 3 April 2026 over a 12 km slick off Gotland (Low).
  - CAROLINE BEZENGI (IMO 9224439, built 2001, Cameroon flag, UK Russia list since 24 February 2026; its UK-named manager is on no list we hold) reportedly ran aground off Oman on 30 June 2026. It was carrying about 800,000 barrels of Russian crude with no recognised Western insurance (MaritimeCyprus, 13 August 2026; one source, Low).
- **Oil money (Medium).**
  - Russia's Finance Ministry plans 8.919 trillion roubles of oil and gas revenue out of 40.283 trillion for 2026, about 22%.
  - January to April actual: 2.298 trillion, about 20% of that period's revenue.
  - 2025: 8.48 trillion (Reuters).
  - This replaces an earlier "about 30%" figure, which described 2024.
- **Fleet size is disputed (Low).** Estimates:
  - 600 to 800 tankers (GSSC, a Lithuanian think tank)
  - 1,423 (Lloyd's List Intelligence, January 2026, including Iran and Venezuela)
  - about 1,500 (Clarksons, as cited by the Financial Times)
- **The buyer stays (High).** KSE names Shandong Yulong Petrochemical among the top three buyers of Russian crude for February to April 2026. The UK listed it on 15 October 2025 and the EU on 23 October 2025 (Reg. 2025/2035).
